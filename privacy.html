Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026

Objectives is a team app for objectives, tasks and comments, published by Ariège, Paris, France ("we"). This policy explains what the app stores, why, and what you can do about it. Questions: noas@ariege-paris.com.

What we collect

  • Account. Your name, email address and password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never visible to us.
  • Workspace content. What you and your team put into the app: company name, departments, objectives, tasks, milestones, comments, time recorded on tasks, and any files or photos you attach to comments.
  • Technical data. Our hosting providers keep standard server logs (IP address, time of request, device type) for security and to run the service.

The app has no advertising, no analytics SDK and no tracking across other apps or websites.

How we use it

  • To run the service: sign you in, show your team's workspace, keep it in sync between devices and send in-app notifications about tasks assigned to you.
  • Ask Claude. When someone taps "Ask Claude" on an objective or task, that item's text (title, what success looks like, current status, the task's details) is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to draft a status update. If your company's admin has connected a sales or advertising data source, recent figures from it are included. Nothing is sent unless you tap the button.
  • Claude connector. If you generate a connector key under Settings › Integrations, Claude can read and update your team's tasks on your behalf through that key, with the permissions you choose. You can revoke the key at any time.

Where it is stored and who processes it

  • Supabase hosts the database, file storage and sign-in (servers in the United States).
  • Expo delivers app updates; the app contacts Expo's servers at launch to check for a newer version.
  • Anthropic processes the text sent through "Ask Claude" or the Claude connector.
  • Windsor.ai provides sales and advertising figures only when a company admin has connected it.

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it with anyone else. Members of your company workspace see what is shared in that workspace, which is the point of the app.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account and your company's workspace exist. Attachments are removed when the comment holding them is deleted.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Settings and tap Delete my account. Your sign-in is removed permanently. Your name stays on past comments and activity so your team's history still makes sense. If you are the last account in the company, the whole workspace, including objectives, tasks, comments and files, is deleted with it. You can also email us and we will do it for you.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to your data, correct it, have it erased, receive a copy, or object to a use. Write to noas@ariege-paris.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in France that is the CNIL.

Children

Objectives is a workplace tool and is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
