Common questions

How do I join my company's workspace?

An admin adds you under Settings › People and gives you an invite code. Open the app, choose Join a company, enter the code, and set your email and password. Confirm the email we send you, then sign in.

I did not get the confirmation email

Check spam, then try signing in: the app offers to resend it. If nothing arrives, email us from the address you signed up with.

I forgot my password

Tap Forgot password on the sign-in screen, or email us and we will send a reset link.

Where do tasks assigned to me show up?

Under My Tasks in the tab bar, and in the bell at the top of the Objectives screen when something is assigned to you or commented on.

How does the work timer work?

On a task, tap Start when you begin and End when you stop. The time adds up across sessions and the objective shows the total of all its tasks. Tap the time to correct it if someone forgot to press End.

What does "Ask Claude" do?

It sends the objective's text to Anthropic's Claude to draft a current status and an accomplishment percentage, which you can edit before keeping. Nothing is sent until you tap the button.

How do I delete my account?

Settings › Delete my account. See the privacy policy for what happens to your data.

How do I get the latest version?

Updates arrive on their own when you open the app. Settings › Check for updates fetches one right away.
