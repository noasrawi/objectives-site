Objectives

One place for a small company's objectives, the tasks behind them, who is responsible, deadlines, milestones and comments. Each company has its own private workspace.

  • Objectives with a target date and a plain-words definition of what success looks like, with an accomplishment percentage.
  • Tasks with priority, department, responsible person, deadline, milestones and a work timer.
  • Comments and attachments on every task, and notifications when something lands on you.
  • Ask Claude drafts a status update from the objective's goal and figures; a connector lets Claude read and update tasks for you.
  • Dark and light themes, on iPhone and on the web.
